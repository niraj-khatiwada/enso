# A little space for your thoughts.

Welcome to **enso**, a quiet home for your Markdown files. This page is a real Markdown file, and you are already editing it: click anywhere and type. Your changes save automatically.

---

## Write in the page

Everything you see is editable in place: headings, *emphasis*, **bold**, `code`, [links](https://example.com), lists, quotes and tables. Press **⌘E** (Ctrl+E on Windows and Linux) to see the Markdown behind the page, and **⌘\\** to put the two side by side.

> Simplicity is the space where ideas take shape.

### Make it yours

- **Typewriter mode** keeps the line you are writing at one height. Drag the dashed guide to move it.
- **Focus** dims everything but the sentence or paragraph you are writing: **⌘⇧D** cycles through the modes.
- **⌘=** and **⌘-** change the text size. The caret's colour, thickness and blink are in Settings (**⌘,**).
- **⌘⇧I** inserts an image; paste a screenshot to save it next to your notes.
- In a table, **Tab** moves between cells and the toolbar adds rows and columns.

### Everything starts with a file

Your files stay on your computer as ordinary `.md` files. enso never rewrites a file you have not edited, and an edit changes only the text you touched.
